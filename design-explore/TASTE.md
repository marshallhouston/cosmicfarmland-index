# Taste rules, from the page owner's own reactions

These come from the owner reacting to five built pages. They are binding.
Where they conflict with anything else, these win.

## Banned outright

- **No conceptual or explanatory copy.** No sentence whose job is to explain the
  metaphor, justify the design, or state the idea. The owner called this out twice
  by name as "pure ai slop":
  - "the same instrument that tells you a star is mostly hydrogen tells you a leaf is
    mostly chlorophyll. one physics, one answer, no scale change. that collapse is the
    whole idea."
  - "herbarium of pressed light"
  If a line exists to make the concept legible, delete it. The design carries the idea
  or the idea is not worth having.
- **No invented tagline under the wordmark.** The owner strongly disliked the generic
  line sitting under COSMIC FARMLAND. Only the real tagline is allowed, and only if it
  earns its place: "apps, tools, and writing. entrypoint to the cosmic farmland"
- **Never the phrase "grows software like crops"**, in any form.
- **No Title Case.** App names and all UI text stay lowercase.
- **No border or frame drawn around the page.** The owner found it odd and pointless.
- **No em-dashes or en-dashes.** No time estimates.

## Required

- **Beige or cream paper ground with simple black type.** The owner said this
  independently about two different variants. It is the strongest positive signal in
  the set. It should not feel "computer-y".
- **Sparse.** Visual busyness is the number one complaint. On a dense page the owner
  reported not knowing where to look or where to go. One clear focal point per screen.
  Empty space is the feature.
- **Faint ruled lines are welcome.** The owner liked them: reads as a notebook or a
  page, personal rather than machine.
- **If drawings or specimens appear, they must be visibly different from each other.**
  Nine near-identical illustrations was a specific complaint.
- **Lowercase, plainspoken, few words.**

## Allowed and liked, use with restraint

- The magenta-to-cyan light gradient is DEMOTED. The owner called it interesting on
  first sight but judged it "generic ai" on both pages that used it. Do not build a
  page around a soft glowing wash. If light appears at all it must be hard, physical,
  and doing a job, never a decorative background bloom.
- What the owner actually liked in the lit register page was the STRUCTURE: an honest
  measured column whose value is really computed and really changes down the page.
  Keep that instinct, drop the glow.
- Intrigue is good. The owner liked not immediately knowing what a page was, as long
  as the page stayed legible and calm. Withhold rather than explain, but never at the
  cost of clarity about what to click.

## Content

Real content only: the nine app names and their real blurbs, the toolshed, the counts.
Nothing invented to fill space.
