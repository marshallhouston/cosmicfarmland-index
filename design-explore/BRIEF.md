# Content brief: cosmicfarmland.wtf apex landing

One person's working farm of apps and tools, shown as a living catalog rather than a resume.
Visitors: developers and product people who followed a link and want to see what else lives here.
Success: they click through to a live app, or to a source repo on GitHub.
Never use the phrase "grows software like crops". The page owner dislikes it. Do not use it in any form.
Voice is lowercase and plainspoken, never corporate. Not a SaaS landing page: no pricing tiers,
no growth metrics, no testimonials.

## Apps (all live, all link out)
1. marshallhouston.wtf - Writing about the cosmic farmland. [writing, essays]
2. Lenny Explorer - A personal archive reader for Lenny's Newsletter posts and podcast transcripts. [search, product]
3. Anthology '77 - An interactive archive of the Grateful Dead's legendary Spring 1977 tour. Map every show, read every setlist, hear the tapes. [music, archive]
4. Systems Playground - Interactive teaching tool for Donella H. Meadows' systems thinking: stocks, flows, feedback loops, delays, and the 12 leverage points. [systems, learning, interactive]
5. Strategy Wizard - Build a real strategy, an action agenda, not a wish list. Guided, with an inline critic, based on Rumelt's Good Strategy / Bad Strategy. [strategy, critic, interactive]
6. Bobby Bowser Arcade - A 10-cabinet arcade of coding-craft minigames. Defeat bad practices, collect good ones. [arcade, games, interactive]
7. Octopus Antipatterns - A personal study site for the 36 antipatterns in The Octopus Organization. Browse by category, drill with recall quizzes, and run a spot-it diagnostic on your own org. [study, patterns]
8. Golf Course Designer - Shape nine holes of dirt, then watch a simulated field of real handicaps expose what you built. Every number on screen is a replayed round, not a rating. [golf, simulation]
9. Golf Record - Every round from my GHIN history: scoring trends, hole-by-hole splits, and where the strokes actually go. [golf, data]
