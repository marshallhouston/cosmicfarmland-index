# The direction: HERBARIUM SHEET

Build the apex landing for cosmicfarmland.wtf as a herbarium sheet.

The page is cream card stock with visible fiber, run to the edges of the viewport with
no frame or border drawn around it. Black ink. No other colour unless one accent earns
a single use. It must not feel computer-y: the reader should think a person made this
sheet, not that a system rendered it.

Nine specimens are mounted on the sheet, one per app, each held down with paper hinges.
Every specimen is a genuinely different organism: different growth habit, leaf shape,
seed head, stem weight, overall size and posture on the card. Some broad and low, some
tall and spindly, some just a root, a pod, or a blade of grass. Nine distinct things,
never nine variations of one silhouette.

Each specimen carries a small accession label in printed furniture: number, name, date,
and the app's real blurb, in lowercase. The label is the link. The toolshed is a
separate list further down the sheet, quieter than the specimens.

A herbarium sheet is mostly empty card, and that restraint is the design. Leave far more
space than feels safe. One specimen or one clear group leads each screenful; everything
else recedes.

Not one word explains the sheet. No thesis line, no tagline under the wordmark, no
sentence about what any of this means. The specimens and their labels carry everything.

The name is cosmic farmland, so something cosmic belongs in the sheet, but it must be
the same object rather than a second idea laid on top, and it must never be announced
in copy.
