Notes to myself before Sunday's round

Written the morning of the final round, two shots in front, before any of what follows happened. Unedited.

Mentally

absolutely locked in. My goal of being the toughest mentally out on the course gave me courage to pursue, accept outcomes, and focus on the process.

It was hot as fuck out there, and I knew that it would make some people melt; not me bc I embraced it. Hydrated a ton and followed my eating plan.

When the wind picked up with 7 holes to play, I told myself, “this is great. Everyone else has to deal with it. It’s a new challenge that you live for,” and gosh that helped me see it as a positive vs frustrating. I was sooo drained for the couple hours afterwards.

My goal doesn’t change today. Be the toughest mentally out on the course. Embrace the process: joy and gratitude to be outside moving my body and open to connection with others (thank volunteers, celebrate good shots from other players), lock in on shot tactic, target, and strategy (where's trouble, where's safe miss), commit to a shot and visualize, pre-shot routine, execute (step away and reset if not full commitment), accept results, and adjust moving forward as needed.

Do i want to win? fuck yes. but that's an outcome i don't control. it's me vs. the course. all i can control is how i show up.

Will i be disappointed if i don't win? it depends. was it b/c i was playing out of fear? inability to accept adversity (it will come... just depends on how i respond)? playing not to lose vs. pursuing and playing my game? or did i focus on my mental goal and process and not get the outcome i wanted? i will not be disappointed if i do all the things in my control.

Physically

definitely sore (expected). ran through a nice recovery routine last night, and i'm currently icing. stretching, eating well (yogurt, chia seeds, and peaches from CSA fruit share + lentil stew and rice is my jam).

warm up properly (range is for moving body, not hitting perfect shots) today, and I'll be good to go. 9am tee off. final group. playing with the guys in 2nd and 3rd (two shots back).

Emotionally

So fucking proud. Elated to put myself in a position to accomplish my goals. This is _exactly_ where I wanted to be. Learning opportunity: about myself, my game, how i respond to adversity, how I go pursue the best version myself with something I want to do.

No matter what happens today, I've put myself in the exact position I want to be. I'll learn a lot.

I'm grateful to have played with some elite golfers this year (women's 67th best amateur, former d1 golfer, other top amateurs) b/c it gave me confidence to just play my game and be comfortable in my own skin. this is a me vs. course scenario.

Who's on the board

By default the board is the live top 12: after every hole it holds whoever is actually in the top 12 at that moment, so players drop in and out as they play. Pick names or a preset instead to pin a fixed set, and every section on this page follows the choice. Either way positions are computed against all 39 players who finished, so a pinned pair still shows their real place in the tournament. Ties at the cut are all kept, so the board sometimes carries more than 12.

Casey Pribble withdrew during the round and is not tracked. Niko Evans started on the 10th tee; his card is rotated into the order he actually played, so "thru 9" means his ninth hole for everyone.
