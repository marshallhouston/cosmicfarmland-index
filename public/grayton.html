✧ the design system

Grayton Beach

Everything on cosmicfarmland.wtf is sampled out of one photograph: a hand-painted welcome sign on County Road 30A, in Grayton Beach, Florida. The palette, the lettering, the texture and the slogan all come off this board.

the sign treatment

One .glyph per letter: a cream text-stroke painted behind a gradient fill, with a fixed rotation and offset on each letter so no two sit on the same baseline. Fixed, not random — the wordmark is identical on every load. That is the difference between hand-painted and a font with an effect on it.

use it

The whole system is one plain stylesheet — tokens, type, the four atmosphere layers, the sign treatment and the interface chrome. No build step, no framework. Link it and set a theme: 

<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://cosmicfarmland.wtf/grayton.css">
<html data-theme="dark">   <!-- or "light"; board is the default -->
